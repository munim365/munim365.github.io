Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · applies to the Munim365 app (com.sonanchaltech.munim365) and this website.

Munim365 (“we”, “us”) is made by Sonanchal Tech Private Limited, India. This policy explains what information the app keeps, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how you can have it deleted. We wrote it to be read in a few minutes.

In short: we keep only what you enter into the app and what is needed to sign you in. Your business data is visible only to your own login. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information we keep

Your account

  • From Google sign-in: your email address, name, profile picture link and Google account ID (see section 2). We never see your Google password.
  • From you: your mobile number and your business details (business name, address, city, GSTIN, bank and UPI details you choose to print on bills, terms, and the logo and stamp images you upload).
  • Subscription: the date until which your account can add and change records (the “paid-until” date).

Your business records

Everything you enter: parties (customers and suppliers) with their names, phone numbers, addresses and GSTIN; ledger entries and balances; bills, payments and credit return bills; products, batches, stock, prices and stock value. This can include information about other people (your parties). You are responsible for having their permission to keep it, as you would in a paper ledger.

Contacts (only if you use it)

When you add a party, you can fill the phone number from your phone's contacts. The app then asks for permission and reads only the one contact you pick. It does not read or upload your contacts list.

Photos (only if you use it)

If you add a business logo or stamp, the app reads only the image you pick from your gallery. It cannot use the camera.

On your phone only

Your login is stored encrypted on your phone. PDFs and Excel files you create are saved on your phone and shared only when you choose to share them. “Log out” removes these files from the phone.

App updates

When you open the app, it asks our app-update service (Expo, expo.dev) whether a newer version of the app's screens is available, and downloads it if there is one. This check sends only technical details: the app version, that the phone runs Android, and a random ID created for this installation (so updates can be counted). It sends none of your account or business data.

What we do not collect

No location, no microphone or camera, no advertising ID, no tracking across other apps, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not read your SMS or call logs.

2. Google user data (Google Sign-In)

Munim365 uses Google Sign-In to create your account and sign you in. This section explains exactly what we receive from your Google account and what we do with it.

  • What we access: only your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID, through the basic “openid”, “email” and “profile” permissions you approve on Google's sign-in screen. We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service, and we never see your Google password.
  • How we use it: to create and identify your Munim365 account, to keep your business data private to your login, to show your login email in the app (Business profile), and to contact you about your account or a support request. The profile picture link is kept by our sign-in service as Google sends it; the app does not display or use the picture.
  • Storage and protection: stored with your account by our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India; sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS); access limited by database rules to your own login and to our support team when you ask for help.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, except the hosting provider above that stores it to run the app, or when the law requires it. We do not use it for advertising.
  • No AI use: we do not use Google user data to train or improve artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and we do not use Google APIs to create, generate or share any AI content.
  • No human reading: people at Sonanchal Tech Private Limited do not read your data unless you ask us for help, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
  • Retention and deletion: kept while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account (Business profile → Delete my account, or these steps), it is deleted within 30 days. You can also remove Munim365's access at any time in your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access.
  • Limited Use: Munim365's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your business private to your login.
  • To store, show and sync your records on every phone where you sign in with the same account.
  • To create the bills, statements, reports and Excel files you ask for.
  • To manage your trial and subscription, and to help you when you contact customer support.
  • To keep the service secure and working (for example, to investigate errors you report).

We use your information only to run the app for you. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell it.

4. Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Supabase stores the data on servers in Mumbai, India. Database rules allow each login to read and change only its own business.
  • Google handles sign-in, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Expo (expo.dev) delivers app updates; it receives only the technical details described under “App updates”, never your business records.
  • WhatsApp, email or other apps receive a bill, statement or message only when you tap Share or Send.
  • Our support team can look at your account only to answer your request, to renew your subscription or when the law requires it.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except if required by law (for example, a valid order from a court or government authority).

5. Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to each business is limited to its own login by rules in the database. Your login on the phone is kept in encrypted storage. No system is perfectly safe; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and records while your account exists, including after a subscription ends (so you can still view and export them). When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days; backup copies expire within a further 30 days. We may keep records we are required by law to keep (for example, invoices for payments you made to us) for the period the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: view and edit your data in the app at any time.
  • Get a copy: Business profile → Export to Excel.
  • Delete: in the app, Business profile → Delete my account, or follow these steps.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the app and ask us to delete your account.
  • Complain: write to our Grievance Officer (below). You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

Munim365 is a business app for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly keep data about children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
