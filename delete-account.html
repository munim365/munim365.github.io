Delete your Munim365 account

Munim365 by Sonanchal Tech Private Limited · app com.sonanchaltech.munim365

You can ask us to delete your Munim365 account and all its data at any time. Our customer care team does the deletion after checking the request comes from you.

Before you ask: deletion can't be undone. If you want a copy of your records, open the app → Business profile → Export to Excel first.

Option 1: from the app (fastest)

  1. Open Munim365 and tap your business card at the top of the Home tab (Business profile).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Contact customer care.
  4. Choose Request on WhatsApp (your request and account details are typed in; tap Send) or Call customer care.

Option 2: without the app

Send “Please delete my Munim365 account” with your login email and registered mobile number to:

  • WhatsApp: +91 77460 73847
  • Phone: +91 77460 73847
  • Email: adarshforpi@gmail.com

Please write from your registered mobile number or email, so we can confirm it is you.

What happens next

  1. We confirm the request with you, using your registered mobile number or email.
  2. Within 30 days we delete your account and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

Your login (Google email and account ID), mobile number and business profile; all parties, ledger entries, bills, payments, credit return bills, products, batches and stock; your logo and stamp images.
