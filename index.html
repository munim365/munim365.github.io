Munim365

Khata, GST billing and stock app for shops in India · made by Sonanchal Tech Private Limited

Munim365 is an Android app that works as a digital munim (accountant) for shop owners, wholesalers and distributors in India. It helps a business keep its accounts, make GST bills and manage its stock from a phone, every day of the year. Everything on this website can be read without signing in.

What Munim365 does

  • Party khata (ledger): keep every customer's and supplier's account with a running CR / DR balance, and record money received or given in seconds.
  • GST bills: make A4 or 80 mm (POS) bills with your logo, stamp, GSTIN and bank / UPI details; GST added on top or included in the price; discounts, extra charges and part payments.
  • Payments and returns: bills post to the party's ledger automatically; record later payments; issue credit return bills that put the goods back in stock.
  • Stock: products with boxes and strips (or any packing), batches, MRP and expiry dates, with alerts for stock that is expiring or out.
  • Share and export: send bills, statements and entries as PDF or WhatsApp messages, print them, and export all your records to Excel.

Who it is for

Small and medium businesses in India: retail shops, medical stores, wholesalers and distributors who want their khata, billing and stock in one simple app instead of paper registers.

How Munim365 uses your Google account

Munim365 uses Google Sign-In only to create your Munim365 account and to let you sign in on your phones. From Google it receives just your name, email address and profile picture link (the basic “openid”, “email” and “profile” permissions). It does not ask for or read your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

  • Your email address is shown in the app as your login and is used to identify your account and to contact you about it.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone except our hosting provider, which stores it only to run the app.
  • Google user data is never used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and Munim365 does not use Google APIs to create, generate or share any AI content.
  • Munim365's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
