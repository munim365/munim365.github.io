Munim365

Maintain your accounts, all 365 days.

Munim365 is a mobile app for shop owners, wholesalers and distributors in India. It keeps your party ledger (CR / DR), bills with GST, credit return bills, stock with batches and expiry dates, and lets you share bills and statements as PDF or on WhatsApp, and export everything to Excel.

Get the app

Android: on Google Play (search “Munim365”). Sign in with your Google account; a new business gets a 14-day free trial.
